# Bezos drone art direction

The overworld drone is a logistics auditor and a visual premonition of the Bezos encounter. It should read as warehouse authority with a human ego trapped inside it, rather than as a consumer quadcopter or a military gunship.

## Runtime composition

- Artwork: `res://assets/mockups/bezos_drone_v2.png`
- Presentation: linear-filtered HD cutout at `0.125` scale
- Procedural layers: moving cyan inspection cone, pulsing scanner eye, blinking status light, hovering shadow
- Interaction area: `190 × 150`, centred beneath the full silhouette
- The encounter dialogue and progression remain independent from the visual composition.

## Generation record

Mode: built-in image generation, default generation mode.

Prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept
>
> Asset type: transparent game character / encounter sprite for a modern illustrated top-down political satire
>
> Primary request: create a completely custom hostile delivery drone that visually foreshadows a cybernetic billionaire warehouse overlord, not a generic consumer quadcopter
>
> Subject: a compact predatory logistics drone with four clearly readable rotor arms; its central armored pod subtly resembles a polished bald chrome head, with one cold cyan scanner eye and an unnervingly perfect mechanical grin; below it, an armored cardboard parcel functions like an insect abdomen, clamped into metal jaws; a narrow receipt/consent ribbon trails from a small printer slot; warehouse conveyor rollers, barcode-scanner geometry, tiny audit camera, yellow safety guards, and red status light are integrated into the machine
>
> Style/medium: premium hand-painted game sprite, stylized realism, slightly caricatural and darkly funny, matching a high-detail modern decaying world rather than pixel art; grounded industrial materials, not sleek science fiction
>
> Composition/framing: single isolated drone, centered, front three-quarter view from slightly above to match a top-down adventure game; strong compact silhouette; all four rotors and the hanging parcel fully visible; generous transparent padding; designed to remain readable when reduced to roughly 120–150 screen pixels
>
> Lighting/mood: hard warehouse inspection light, threatening but absurd, crisp highlights and controlled shadows on the object only
>
> Color palette: gunmetal and worn aluminum, dirty corporate yellow safety accents, cardboard brown, cold cyan scanner eye, tiny red warning light
>
> Materials/textures: scratched metal, rubber conveyor rollers, corrugated cardboard, printed receipt paper, greasy joints
>
> Constraints: genuinely transparent background with clean alpha; no floor, no cast shadow, no environment, no border; no words, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no trademarks, no watermark; one drone only; preserve a clear game-sprite silhouette
>
> Avoid: generic white hobby drone, military gunship, futuristic spaceship, cute robot, photoreal product photography, pixel art, extra packages or detached props
